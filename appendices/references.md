# References and Further Reading

This handbook is an original work. The sources below informed it, are cited in the jurisdiction guides or are recommended as companion reading. Statutes and regulations are listed by their operative dates as at early July 2026 unless an entry carries a later date, to which it was verified. **URLs added 15 October 2026 (KST).** An entry marked **[opened]** was read at that address on 15 October 2026 (KST) in the whole-account review or by its delegated reviewers; an entry marked **[address]** gives the publisher's canonical address, not opened in that pass. The dated status of every instrument, with its next check, is in [CURRENCY.md](../CURRENCY.md); verify current status before reliance.

## International frameworks

- OECD, Recommendation of the Council on Artificial Intelligence (OECD AI Principles), as revised 2024. Source of the AI system definition adopted across national laws. **[address]** [https://legalinstruments.oecd.org/en/instruments/OECD-LEGAL-0449](https://legalinstruments.oecd.org/en/instruments/OECD-LEGAL-0449)
- NIST, Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0, 2023) and Generative AI Profile (NIST AI 600-1, 2024). The de facto US baseline; compliance supports an affirmative defense under Texas TRAIGA. **[opened]** [https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework); **[address]** [https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.AI.600-1](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.AI.600-1)
- ISO/IEC 42001:2023, Artificial intelligence management system. The certifiable management-system standard most closely aligned with the operating discipline in Part 3. **[opened]** [https://www.iso.org/standard/42001](https://www.iso.org/standard/42001)
- ISO/IEC 23894:2023, AI risk management guidance. **[address]** [https://www.iso.org/standard/77304.html](https://www.iso.org/standard/77304.html)
