# References and Further Reading

This handbook is an original work. The sources below informed it, are cited in the jurisdiction guides or are recommended as companion reading. Statutes and regulations are listed by their operative dates as at early July 2026; verify current status before reliance.

## International frameworks

- OECD, Recommendation of the Council on Artificial Intelligence (OECD AI Principles), as revised 2024. Source of the AI system definition adopted across national laws.
- NIST, Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0, 2023) and Generative AI Profile (2024). The de facto US baseline; compliance supports an affirmative defense under Texas TRAIGA.
- ISO/IEC 42001:2023, Artificial intelligence management system. The certifiable management-system standard most closely aligned with the operating discipline in Part 3.
- ISO/IEC 23894:2023, AI risk management guidance.

## European Union

- Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (Artificial Intelligence Act), in force 1 August 2024, phased application.
- Digital Omnibus on AI: Commission proposal 19 November 2025; political agreement 7 May 2026; European Parliament endorsement 16 June 2026; Council final approval 29 June 2026; **entered into force 27 July 2026**. Defers Annex III high-risk obligations to 2 December 2027 and Annex I to 2 August 2028.
- European Commission, Code of Practice on marking and labelling AI-generated content (June 2026).
- Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR), actively enforced against AI processing by data protection authorities.

## United States

- Executive Order, "Ensuring a National Policy Framework for Artificial Intelligence" (11 December 2025), and subsequent federal AI policy actions of March and June 2026.
- Colorado SB 26-189 (Automated Decision-Making Technology, signed 14 May 2026, obligations from 1 January 2027), repealing and replacing SB 24-205 (2024).
- Texas HB 149, Responsible Artificial Intelligence Governance Act (TRAIGA), effective 1 January 2026.
- California SB 53 (Transparency in Frontier Artificial Intelligence Act) and AB 2013 (training data transparency), effective 1 January 2026; SB 942 (AI Transparency Act), operative 2 August 2026; CPPA automated decision-making regulations, phasing through 2027.
- Illinois HB 3773 (Human Rights Act amendments on employment AI), effective 1 January 2026; New York City Local Law 144 (2023); New York RAISE Act (signed 19 December 2025, effective 1 January 2027); Utah AI Policy Act.
- NIST AI RMF, above, as the multistate compliance anchor.

## Republic of Korea

- Framework Act on the Development of Artificial Intelligence and the Establishment of a Foundation for Trust (AI Framework Act / AI Basic Act), Act No. 20676 (promulgated 21 January 2025), effective 22 January 2026.
- Enforcement Decree, Presidential Decree No. 36053, effective 22 January 2026.
- Ministry of Science and ICT guidance on generative AI labeling and high-impact determination, evolving through the 2026 grace period.
- Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA), coordinated with the Framework Act by the Enforcement Decree.

## Vietnam

- Law on Artificial Intelligence, No. 134/2025/QH15 (passed 10 December 2025), effective 1 March 2026.
- Decree 142/2026/ND-CP (30 April 2026, effective 1 May 2026), implementing risk classification, transition notices and the AI sandbox.
- Law on Personal Data Protection, No. 91/2025/QH15, effective 1 January 2026.
- Law on Digital Technology Industry, No. 71/2025/QH15 (AI chapter superseded by Law 134); Decree 353/2025/ND-CP (digital technology sandbox).

## Australia

- Australian Government, National AI Plan (December 2025), confirming the existing-law approach.
- National AI Centre, Guidance for AI Adoption (October 2025): the six essential practices, in Foundations and Implementation editions; AI policy guide and template.
- Australia's AI Ethics Principles (voluntary, OECD-aligned).
- Corporations Act 2001 (Cth); ASIC Report 798, Beware the gap: governance arrangements in the face of AI innovation (October 2024); ASIC v Bekier (Liability Judgment) [2026] FCA 196 (directors' use of AI on board papers).
- Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), including automated decision-making disclosure obligations from 10 December 2026 and the statutory tort for serious invasions of privacy (2025); OAIC guidance on generative AI and commercially available AI products.
- Cyber Security Act 2024 (Cth); Security of Critical Infrastructure Act 2018 (Cth); ASD, APRA and ASIC AI-and-cyber guidance (May 2026).
- Work Health and Safety Amendment (Digital Work Systems) Act 2026 (NSW); Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth); Copyright Act 1968 (Cth); Australian Consumer Law.
- AICD and UTS Human Technology Institute, A Director's Guide to AI Governance, Version 2 (June 2026). A leading national treatment of board AI oversight and recommended reading, particularly for Australian directors; companion AICD publications on cyber security governance, data governance and board minutes and AI.

## Practice literature

- Gradient Institute, Risk Analysis Techniques for Governed LLM-based Multi-Agent Systems (2025), on agentic and multi-agent risk.
- Published corporate transparency reports on AI adoption and governance, an emerging genre worth benchmarking against; Commonwealth Bank of Australia's Our Approach to Adopting AI (December 2025) is a prominent example.
- Author's related work: the Slow AI, Final Liability and Informed Intent doctrines are developed further at substack.com/@redcaps and github.com/rolldabones, including the book in progress, Final Liability Rests with the Human.
