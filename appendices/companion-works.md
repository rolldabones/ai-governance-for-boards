# Companion Works

**This handbook is one of four repositories that together form a complete AI governance stack: doctrine for the board, a build instrument for the practitioner, a working method for the individual professional and structured programs for delivery. Each stands alone. They are designed to be used together.**

## How the stack fits together

The board directs, the enterprise builds, the professional practices and the programs deliver. This handbook tells a board what to require: a register, named owners, a differentiated appetite, authorization gates, assurance. The GRC Workbook is the instrument management uses to construct what the board required; its thirty-seven item Governance Test in Module 18 is the audit of whether the construction is real. The Slow AI Kitchen is the discipline each professional applies so that the controls exist at the level where work actually happens, one task and one gate at a time. The Academy programs are the structured paths an organization can engage to move through all three with expert support.

The dependency runs downward and the evidence runs upward. A board resolution without an operating capability is aspiration; the Workbook builds the capability. A capability without daily practice is paperwork; the Kitchen supplies the practice. Task-level evidence packs roll up through the Workbook's artifacts into the reporting this handbook teaches boards to demand.

## One doctrine set, three altitudes

The three doctrines are stated once and expressed at three altitudes. The formulations differ by audience; the doctrine does not. A reader who encounters more than one repository should reconcile them as follows.

### Slow AI

- **Board altitude (this handbook).** Three tests a system must pass before and after deployment: governed, explainable, auditable. Adoption pace is an output of governance capacity, not an input the board sets by ambition.
- **Enterprise altitude (GRC Workbook, Module 9).** Calibrated release: capability is deployed at the rate the organization, the people and the affected parties can absorb without losing the capacity to govern, with release tiers, gating criteria and fallback positions an independent reviewer can inspect.
- **Task altitude (Slow AI Kitchen).** The twelve-step method itself: manual first pass before AI touches the work, bounded AI use, verification before release. The Kitchen's three risk tiers are the task-level expression of the enterprise release tiers.

These are the same constraint measured at different scales. The board's three tests are satisfied only where the enterprise releases in governed increments, and the enterprise can release in governed increments only where individual work passes through gates.

### Informed Intent

The two five-part formulations in this suite answer different questions and are both required for a complete authorization.

- **This handbook states the five elements of the authorization instrument**, the *what*: purpose, scope of action, authority, human checkpoints and exit. These are the contents of the document that gates deployment.
- **The GRC Workbook (Module 10) states the five conditions of a valid authorization**, the *who*: the authorizing human must have the knowledge, the evidence, the authority, the time and the permission to authorize. These are the tests of whether the signature counts.

An instrument with all five elements signed by a human lacking the five conditions is administrative theater. A fully qualified human signing an instrument missing elements has authorized something undefined. Complete Informed Intent is a five-condition human executing a five-element instrument. In the Kitchen, the same doctrine operates as Gate 4 (Bounded AI Use) and the pre-deployment discipline of Steps 1 and 2.

### Final Liability

The formulation is uniform across all four repositories: a named human carries the consequence of every consequential AI outcome, in advance, with no gap in the chain. This handbook implements it as the ownership chain (system owner, decision owner, board accountability). The Workbook implements it as the named human plus evidence pack plus corrective capacity (Module 11). The Kitchen implements it as Gate 5, Name the Chef de Partie. The Academy renders it in every Client document. It is the closing line of each repository because it is the premise of all of them.

## Using the suite with clients and advisors

The suite is sequenced for engagement as well as for reading. A typical path: this handbook orients the board (directors read Part 1 and Part 6; the general counsel reads everything), the Governance Test in Workbook Module 18 baselines the current state, the gap drives the build through the Workbook's modules, the Kitchen's workshop trains the workforce that must live inside the controls and the jurisdiction guides in Part 5 set the compliance calendar. The Academy templates package that path into six programs of increasing depth. Organizations working without an advisor can follow the identical sequence; every artifact it produces is defined in the free repositories.
