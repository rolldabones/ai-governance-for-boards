# Board Questions Bank

**Questions directors can ask verbatim. Organized by governance element, with the doctrine each question serves. A director does not need to know the answer; a director needs to recognize a non-answer.**

## Strategy and appetite

1. Do we have a clear, written understanding of the organization's strategy with AI, and does every material AI investment trace to it?
2. What is our risk appetite for AI use, where is it written, and how does it differ across technologies, use cases and stakeholder groups?
3. Have we communicated to management what we see as the key AI opportunities and the risks to be managed?
4. What did the baseline inventory show about how AI and data are actually used today, and what did it change in the plan?
5. Do we have agreed measures and indicators of AI progress and ultimate success, including return on investment?
6. What will we not use AI for, regardless of return? (Doctrine: values answer the lawful-but-unwise cases.)

## Accountability and structure

7. Who is the named executive accountable for AI governance, and do they have the authority and resources the role requires? (Doctrine: Final Liability.)
8. For each of our five most consequential AI systems, who owns the system and who owns the decisions it informs? Answer with names.
9. Has management implemented an effective governance structure for oversight and decision-making on AI use, including high-risk uses? Should a management-led AI committee exist, or be simplified?
10. Do we receive timely, comprehensive, plain-language reporting on AI use across the organization, and when did we last renegotiate what it covers?
11. When did an AI issue last escalate to this board, and was that because escalation works or because nothing has surfaced?

## Risk and controls

12. What steps give us confidence we are meeting our legal and regulatory obligations for AI and the associated data collection, storage and use, in every jurisdiction we operate in?
13. Does our risk management framework adequately address AI-related risks, and does it differentiate between high-risk and low-risk applications?
14. What controls are in place to manage agentic risks specifically, and has the suspension mechanism for each agent been tested rather than described? (Doctrine: Informed Intent.)
15. Do our existing privacy, data governance, cyber and procurement policies address AI, and when were they last reviewed for it?
16. For our most critical AI vendor: what testing preceded adoption, what are our contractual notification and testing rights, what is the fallback on failure and what would exit cost? (Doctrine: Slow AI, reversibility.)
17. Which systems currently in production could we not explain to a regulator or an affected person tomorrow, and what is the remediation plan for each?

## Culture and enablers

18. Do we have a strategy for understanding the impact of our AI implementation on internal and external stakeholders, and have we consulted where required or prudent?
19. Do we have a comprehensive view of underlying data volume and quality, and how does that constrain our AI effectiveness?
20. Have our cyber security and data governance processes, practices and policies been updated for AI implementation, including rapid patching and privileged access management inside AI environments?
21. Are current technology platforms and data flows fit for purpose for safe and responsible AI governance, including the transparency and explanation the architecture must enable?
22. What sanctioned AI tools do staff have, what does monitoring show about unsanctioned use and what does training cover?

## Value and returns

23. For each of our three largest AI investments: what specific improvement was it meant to deliver, and what do the metrics show now?
24. What proportion of the intended user base actually uses each deployed capability?
25. Are usage-based consumption costs, staff training and quality assurance inside the cost model, or are we comparing full benefits to license-only costs?
26. What AI initiative did we retire in the last year on evidence, and if the answer is none, why should we believe we are measuring?

## The board's own use of AI

27. Is board and committee use of AI recorded in the AI register and governed by an agreed policy?
28. Are we individually satisfied that we are not substituting machine summaries for our own review and interrogation of board papers?
29. Does our use of AI in governance blur lines of accountability or undermine confidence in management, and would we know?

## Jurisdiction triggers

30. Which of our systems fall within the EU AI Act's high-risk categories, and what is the build plan against the December 2027 and August 2028 deadlines, with transparency duties live from August 2026?
31. Which systems constitute high-impact or generative AI under Korea's Framework Act, is labeling implemented and do we meet a domestic agent threshold?
32. Have our Vietnam-facing systems been risk-classified before operation, were transition notices filed and are we tracking the pending high-risk list and penalty decrees?
33. Which US states' AI statutes reach our operations, and are we building to the strictest applicable standard rather than waiting for federal preemption?
34. In Australia, are we ready for the December 2026 automated decision-making privacy disclosures, and is our program aligned to the six essential practices?
