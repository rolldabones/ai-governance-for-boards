# AI Governance for Boards

**A doctrine-first handbook for directors overseeing artificial intelligence, with jurisdiction guides for the EU, the US, Korea, Vietnam and Australia.**

Version 1.4.1 | 13 August 2026 | License: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0

---

## What this repository is

This is a working reference for board directors, general counsel and governance professionals who must oversee AI adoption without becoming AI engineers. It is organized around three owned doctrines developed in practice:

- **Slow AI**: governed, explainable, auditable AI. Speed of adoption is subordinate to the ability to explain, audit and reverse what has been adopted.
- **Final Liability**: every material outcome of an AI system attaches to a named human owner. No output, decision or harm is orphaned to "the model."
- **Informed Intent**: pre-deployment authorization that specifies what an AI system or agent is permitted to do, on whose authority, within what scope and with what exit.

The handbook translates these doctrines into board-level obligations, an operating discipline, a value measurement framework and five jurisdiction guides. It is designed for a global audience. The regulatory content is current as at early July 2026 and dated throughout; verify current status before relying on any specific provision.

## Where this handbook sits

This handbook is the board layer of a four-repository suite. The board directs, the enterprise builds, the professional practices and the programs deliver:

- **AI Governance for Boards** (this handbook): what the board must own, ask and refuse.
- **[GRC Workbook](https://github.com/rolldabones/grc-workbook)**: the eighteen-module instrument, built on the OCEG GRC Capability Model 3.5, with which management constructs the capability this handbook presupposes, closing on a thirty-seven item Governance Test.
- **[Slow AI Kitchen](https://github.com/rolldabones/slow-ai-kitchen)**: the twelve-step, ten-gate method by which individual professionals and teams apply the doctrines to real work, with a workshop guide.
- **[AI Governance Academy](https://github.com/rolldabones/AI-Governance-Academy)**: engagement templates for six structured delivery programs built on these materials.

Each repository stands alone. [Companion works](appendices/companion-works.md) maps how they fit together, reconciles the doctrine formulations across them and provides reading paths by role.

## Who this is for

Directors and officers of companies of any size, in any jurisdiction, who carry legal accountability for AI adoption. It is equally intended for the general counsel, chief risk officers and company secretaries who prepare boards to discharge that accountability. Nothing here requires technical expertise. Everything here assumes the reader can be held liable.

## How to use it

Read Part 1 first. The doctrine layer is short and everything else depends on it. Part 2 establishes why the board, not management alone, owns AI oversight. Part 3 is the operating discipline: appetite, accountability, controls, culture. Part 4 addresses whether AI investments are returning value. Part 5 is jurisdiction-specific. Part 6 distills globally applicable best practices, board questions, red flags and a checklist for smaller organizations. When the board's questions turn into management's build, hand the work to the [GRC Workbook](https://github.com/rolldabones/grc-workbook); when the build turns into daily practice, train with the [Slow AI Kitchen](https://github.com/rolldabones/slow-ai-kitchen).

## Design principles

1. **Doctrine before checklist.** Checklists decay; doctrines transfer. Each jurisdiction guide maps local law back to the three doctrines so the reader carries one mental model across borders.
2. **Named humans, not diffuse committees.** Wherever this handbook says "accountability," it means a named human owner with authority, resources and consequences.
3. **Reversibility as a control.** An AI deployment the organization cannot switch off, exit or explain is a governance failure regardless of its performance.
4. **Jurisdiction guides are snapshots.** Law dates are stated in the text. Anything undated should be treated as unverified.
5. **One doctrine set across the suite.** The doctrines here are stated at board altitude. The [GRC Workbook](https://github.com/rolldabones/grc-workbook) states them at enterprise altitude and the [Slow AI Kitchen](https://github.com/rolldabones/slow-ai-kitchen) at task altitude. The reconciliation is in [Companion works](appendices/companion-works.md).
