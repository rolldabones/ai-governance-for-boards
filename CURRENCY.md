# Currency register

**v1.6.0 · 15 October 2026 (KST) · one row per instrument this handbook relies on: what it is, where the handbook cites it, its status tag, the date to which it was last verified, the primary address and the next check.**

This register replaces nothing in the guides' prose and restates no figure. The guides keep their analysis; the dates live here, once. A guide's status line binds only in the respects it names (see the [currency and status convention](05-jurisdictions/comparison.md#currency-and-status-convention)); a row here binds for the instrument it names. The four status tags are those of the convention: **Binding law**, **Regulator guidance**, **Proposed**, **Author's doctrine**, with **Enacted, not in effect** and **Unknown** added here because the register needs them. **[opened]** means the address was read on the as-at date; **[address]** means it is the publisher's canonical address, not opened in that pass. The quarterly preemption watch the US guide prescribes is row US-1.

## How this register is maintained

1. A row changes only when the instrument was re-verified at the address given; the as-at date moves with it. A guide sentence that restates a row's date is corrected to point here instead.
2. A correction of substance is struck in the guide and itemized in the [CHANGELOG](CHANGELOG.md); the row records the new position.
3. The weekly account sweep reads the Next check column and reports rows due; it changes nothing.
4. Unknown is a status, not a gap to be filled by inference.
